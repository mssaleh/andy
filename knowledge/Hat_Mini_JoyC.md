## Description

**Hat Mini JoyC** is a joystick controller base designed for StickC/StickC-Plus products. It integrates an XY-axis joystick with a built-in button function and comes with an I2C HY2.0 standard connector for easy expansion with other peripherals. Inside, it houses a 200 mAh lithium-polymer battery and an SK6812 RGB LED. This product can be used for remote control of drones or cars, as well as remote operation of robotic joints, etc.

## Features

- Maximum joystick angle in each direction: 23°
- Total resistance: 10 kΩ
- Center button
- SK6812 programmable RGB LED
- 200 mAh battery
- HY2.0-4P connector

## Includes

- 1 x Hat Mini JoyC
- 1 x Lanyard
- 1 x Hex Key L-Shape 1.5 mm (For M2 Screw)
- 2 x M2 \* 8 mm Screws (pan head, self-tapping)

## Applications

- Drone remote control
- Robotic joint control
- Light dimmer
- Car remote control
